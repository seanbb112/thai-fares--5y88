Before you book

  • Prices come from Google Flights at the time of each run. Confirm on the airline's site before paying.
  • A stopover is two separate tickets. If the first flight is late and you miss the second, the second airline has no duty to rebook you. Leave at least one night, or a long gap.
  • Budget carriers on the second leg often exclude checked bags, so add the bag price before comparing.
  • Gulf routes (Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Sharjah, Kuwait, Bahrain, Saudi): research on 12/10/26 found the DFA advising against non-essential travel to these countries and EASA airspace advice running to 16/11/26. Read the current advice at ireland.ie/dfa before booking anything marked ⚠.
  • China visa-free entry for Irish passports was reported to end 31/12/26. Check before a Beijing or Shanghai stopover that runs into January.
  • Check visa and transit rules for the stopover country on an Irish passport, and Thailand's digital arrival card (TDAC).
  • Hotel and food costs during a stopover are not included in the totals.
